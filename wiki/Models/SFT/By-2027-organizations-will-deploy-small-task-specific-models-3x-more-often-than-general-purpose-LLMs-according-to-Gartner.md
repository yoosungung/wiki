# By 2027, organizations will deploy small, task-specific models 3x more often than general-purpose LLMs according to Gartner.

By 2027, organizations will deploy small, task-specific models 3x more often than general-purpose LLMs according to [Gartner](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gartner?trk=public_post-text). I think 3x is an understatement. The emergence of model routers is a clear signal that users and organizations are acutely aware that not all queries require large frontier models. And the explosion of SOTA open weights models not only gives users more base models to choose from but also more models to fine-tune. Large proprietary models like Claude and GPT models are difficult to fine-tune. Open weights models are much easier. And they are getting smaller and better at an astonishing rate. If open weights models keep improving at the current pace, we will see SLMs far outnumbering LLMs in production scenarios. And for good reasons, because open weights SLMs are: → Cheaper to deploy than LLMs → Can operate in resource-constrained environments → Maintain data privacy → Are significantly easier and cheaper to customize (fine-tune) than LLMs SLMs are going mainstream and we have never been more ready for it at [Fastino Labs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fastinoai?trk=public_post-text). We can’t wait to share what we’ve been working on this summer.
