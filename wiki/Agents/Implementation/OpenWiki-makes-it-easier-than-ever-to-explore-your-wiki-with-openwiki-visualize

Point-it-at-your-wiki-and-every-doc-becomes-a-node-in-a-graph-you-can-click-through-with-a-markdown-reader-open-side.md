# OpenWiki makes it easier than ever to explore your wiki with openwiki visualize

Point it at your wiki and every doc becomes a node in a graph you can click through, with a markdown reader open side…

OpenWiki makes it easier than ever to explore your wiki with openwiki visualize Point it at your wiki and every doc becomes a node in a graph you can click through, with a markdown reader open side by side. It runs locally and updates live as the wiki changes. ➡️ The graph plus the reader makes it easy to check what the agent actually wrote about your codebase and to view generated diagrams. ➡️ With OKF support, your wiki is a set of connections between concepts in your codebase. A graph shows those connections, which helps a lot when you're finding your way around a repo you didn't write. Leave it open during a run and watch the wiki fill in.
